About This Game
\n
\n

\n Surviving South AAH!!!\" is a realistic survival life simulator set in modern South Africa. This is NOT an arcade game. It's a deeply emotional, strategic experience about surviving another day.\n

\n

\n Your Goals: Manage hunger, health, stress, energy, hygiene, hope, money, and relationships while navigating the harsh realities of township life, rural poverty, and urban struggle.\n

\n

\n Key Systems: Job hunting, farming, economic pressure (inflation, taxi strikes, load shedding), family obligations, crime temptation, and mental health crises.\n

\n

\n Multiple Endings: Survival, Success, Migration, Farming Stability, Community Leader, Burnout, Corruption, Prison, Homelessness, or Death.\n

\n

\n This prototype demonstrates the core mechanics and atmosphere. The full game will include 50+ events, 20+ NPCs, farming mechanics, and much more.\n
