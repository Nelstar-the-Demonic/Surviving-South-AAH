Privacy Policy — Surviving South Africa

Effective date: 17 August 2026

Kwantu Nation ("Kwantu Nation", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Surviving South Africa game ("SSA" or "the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Game and how third-party services used by the Game may process information.

1. Information processed by the Game

SSA is primarily a single-player game. Depending on the version, device and features you use, information may be stored locally on your device to preserve game state and settings. The Game does not require you to create an account simply to play.

2. Advertising

The Android version of SSA uses Google AdMob to display advertising, including banner and rewarded advertising.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and advertising interaction information in accordance with Google's policies and applicable law. The exact information processed can depend on your device, operating system, consent choices and the advertising configuration available in your region.

SSA may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Choosing to watch a rewarded advertisement is voluntary.

At the time this policy was published, Firebase Analytics is not represented as an active data-collection service unless and until it is enabled in a released version of the Game. If analytics services are added, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data processing.

5. Third-party services

The Game may use third-party software development kits and services for advertising, distribution, diagnostics, or other technical functionality. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's privacy

SSA is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The Game does not ask players to submit sensitive personal information merely to play. Advertising treatment may also depend on the advertising provider and applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by the Game generally remains on the device until it is deleted by the player, the Game is uninstalled, or relevant storage is cleared. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and applicable law.

8. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to interact with optional rewarded advertisements.
  • You can manage certain advertising and privacy controls through your Android device and Google's available settings.
  • You can uninstall the Game to remove its locally stored application data, subject to normal device backup behaviour.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the Game to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. No electronic storage or internet transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.
