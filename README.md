# Surviving South AAH!!!

A realistic, gritty survival life simulator set in modern South Africa.

## Overview

"Surviving South AAH!!!" is a single-player mobile survival RPG that puts you in the shoes of a struggling South African trying to survive another day. Manage your hunger, health, stress, money, and relationships while navigating the harsh realities of township life, rural poverty, and urban struggle.

This is **not** a game about defeating zombies or collecting points. This is a game about **surviving with dignity**.

## Game Features

### Core Systems
- **Daily Survival Loop**: Wake up, manage stats, make critical decisions, sleep
- **Resource Management**: Money, food, energy, health, stress, hygiene, hope
- **Dynamic Events**: Random encounters, economic changes, weather, social pressure
- **Branching Choices**: Decisions that genuinely affect your outcome and story
- **Multiple Endings**: Survival, success, corruption, migration, burnout, and more

### Gameplay Elements
- **Job Hunting & Hustling**: Legal work, side gigs, informal economy
- **Farming System**: Plant crops, manage livestock, deal with theft and drought
- **Economic Pressure**: Rising costs, inflation, unexpected expenses, debt
- **South African Realism**:
  - Load shedding and electricity outages
  - Taxi transport costs and strikes
  - Social grants and bureaucracy
  - Police interactions and corruption
  - Protest movements and political tension
  - Township, rural, and urban environments

### Character & Relationship System
- Build relationships with NPCs
- Family pressure and obligations
- Community interactions
- Realistic South African characters and dialogue

### Mental Health & Morale
- Stress accumulation affects decision-making
- Hope/morale system impacts resilience
- Burnout mechanics
- Mental health crises

## Technical Details

**Platform**: Mobile-first (Android/iOS optimized)
**Engine**: Built for low-end devices, offline-capable
**Architecture**: Turn-based progression with real-time ambient atmosphere
**Performance**: Lightweight, efficient resource management

## Key Design Principles

1. **Emotional Realism**: The game should make you feel the stress and struggle of survival
2. **No Simplification**: Systems are complex because life is complex
3. **Gritty Aesthetic**: Muted colors, weathered UI, realistic textures
4. **Consequence**: Every choice matters and shapes your story
5. **Hope Amid Struggle**: Progress is possible but never easy
6. **South African Authenticity**: Grounded in real struggles, not stereotypes

## Visual Style
